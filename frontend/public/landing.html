How It Works

1

Registration

Hospitals and Organ Procurement Organizations (OPOs) register donors, recipients, and organs on the blockchain.

2

Verification

Smart contracts automatically verify and match organs with recipients based on predefined criteria.

3

Tracking

The entire process, from reservation to transplant, is tracked in real-time on the secure ledger.
